Session log analysis, Khoi's `runs/try1.npz` (82.7 s, 29.8 FPS, hand detected 88 percent, 2096 frames engaged with a hand):
- Software lag is small: hand to the arm's real position 10 ms (left-right) and 30 ms (up-down). Camera and USB delay is not in the log. Raw jitter when still: 1.0 thousandth of the image. So neither lag nor tracking noise in u and v explains "shaky, hard to follow".
- The depth cue was the problem: raw inverse depth ranged 1.5 to 14.6 (median 2.7, p95 8.5) with 1 to 2 frame spikes (5 -> 11 -> 15 -> 5). The target x sat at the box edge in 49 percent of engaged frames and z at the floor in 67 percent, so the arm ignored the hand for long stretches (the hand covered 0.5 of the image height, the arm only has 0.23 m). The target was clamped with no way back: a dead zone.
- ee-to-target error median 1.7 mm but p95 28 mm, 77 mm near x < 0.15 (the region the expert cannot grasp in).
- Pinch gap: closed median 2.5 cm, open median 5.6 cm; 46 gripper switches in 83 s; the thresholds 3 and 5 cm sit close to the open and closed medians but work.
Changes (tests first, 4 deliberate bugs caught): gains 0.6 / 0.5 / 0.3 (y, z, x), median over 9 frames and a heavier filter on depth, no wind-up at the workspace edge, workspace x from 0.15, the log now stores the full hand landmarks so a session can be replayed offline. Exit crash 139 fixed (MuJoCo viewer thread segfault in glfw.terminate at interpreter shutdown; the process now leaves with os._exit after saving).
Still open: the depth cue is unreliable even smoothed; candidates are depth on keys or dropping depth. Needs one more logged session (the log now has the landmarks).

Second session, Khoi's `runs/try2.npz` (79.6 s, 29.6 FPS, hand detected 59 percent; Khoi: "back felt better, forward hard to navigate, arm still responds but still hard"). Exit crash gone (exit 0).
- Dropouts while engaged: 30 runs, 639 of 2113 frames lost; 15 of 31 began while pinching (fingers curled), only 1 near an image edge. Fix tried: MediaPipe confidences 0.3 (were 0.5). Better: grip with the G key so the hand stays open to the camera.
- Target edge pinning fell (x 26 percent, z 9 percent) and target roughness is now 2 to 3 mm per axis, but the real end-effector was 6 to 7.6 mm rough. Replaying the logged targets through IK and the real physics at live timing reproduced it (baseline 6.6, 6.8, 7.0 mm; IK error median 0.1 mm). A rate limit, a floor margin and a higher orientation weight did not change it, so it is not target speed, floor contact or IK. Cause found: the live loop advanced physics in 50 ms bursts (one 20 Hz control step) while the window refreshes every 33 ms, so the arm moved in jumps. Fix: `Env.substep` (10 substeps equal one step exactly, tested) and the live loop now advances 5 ms at a time in real time.
- Depth cue: on the real landmarks the world-landmark tilt correction was 7 times noisier than the plain image length (noise 0.76 against 0.11 of the median) and its tilt estimate sat at its clip most of the time, so the cue is now the summed image length of four palm segments, median over 9 frames, 0.4 Hz filter. Cost: a 45 degree pitch moves the arm about 6 cm (measured on synthetic landmarks).

Not verified: the live run (the webcam was held by another app), feel, and the 5 of 10 bar, which is Khoi's trial.

Fallback first session, Khoi's `runs/try4.npz` plus two unlogged runs (0 of 12 lifted; 27.8 FPS, hand detected 81-93 percent): the log shows the W/S offset never changed and the G key was never closed in any attempt, while SPACE worked; in attempt 3 the gripper was 1.3 cm from the grasp point and "ready" for 49 percent of the attempt. Cause (claim, consistent with the log, not reproduced by a key press here): the desktop runs ibus with Unikey (Telex), the OpenCV window is a Qt window with QT_IM_MODULE=ibus, and Telex takes letters for composing. Fix: the demo bypasses the input method for its window, letters work in either case, and the panel shows the last key received. The hand control itself reached every part of the y and z range.
